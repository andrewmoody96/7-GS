# Roadmap

Short on purpose. **Now** is being built, **Next** is lined up, **Later** is ideas worth
keeping. Move things as plans change; [RELEASES.md](RELEASES.md) has how releases work.

## Now

- **1.1: launch.** A recap of everything since launch, badges for every account (earned for
  past play too), a season win goal in the guided setup, and one more pass through setup.
- **Email sign-in for real.** Confirm email delivery, then turn `ALLOW_DEV_LOGIN` off on
  Heroku.

## Next

- **Sunday recap email.** Last week's series and record, this week's plan, and any badge
  earned. A reason to come back each week.
- **Badge polish.** A badge's date earned, and a short celebration when a big one lands.

## Later (ideas)

- **Friends or a league:** compare records and series wins with people you invite.
- **Passkey sign-in**, so no code is needed on a device you've used before.
- **More sports** for themes.
